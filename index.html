Oiko

Oiko is a private family app for planning the week: who does drop-off, pickup, dinner and bedtime, and who drives the kids to their activities.

It is a pilot used by one family. It is not open to the public.

A parent can connect their Google Calendar so Oiko can point out when a care task clashes with a meeting. Oiko only reads the calendar. It never changes it.
