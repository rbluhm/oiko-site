Oiko privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who we are

Oiko is a private app that Richard Bluhm runs for his own family. It is a pilot, not a public service. Contact: richard.bluhm@gmail.com.

What Oiko reads from Google

If you connect your Google Calendar, Oiko asks for two read-only permissions:

  • See the list of calendars you subscribe to, so you can choose which calendars Oiko looks at.
  • View events on your calendars, so Oiko can tell when you are busy.

Oiko cannot create, change or delete anything in your calendar.

What Oiko keeps

For events marked busy and for out-of-office events, Oiko keeps the start and end time, whether the event is all day, whether it is out of office, and its title. Events marked free are skipped. Oiko does not keep descriptions, attendees or locations.

Oiko only reads events from about a week back to about six months ahead, and removes older ones.

It also keeps the email address of the Google account you connected, so you can see which account is connected, and which of your calendars you chose, each with a marker of how far it has been read.

When an out-of-office event or a whole day looks like a trip, Oiko suggests it as time away. If you confirm it, it becomes part of the family's plan, with the event's title as its label.

How it is used

Only to show the family's week, to point out when a parent's event clashes with a care task or a drive, and to suggest when a parent is away. Only the parents in the same family can see it.

Where it is stored

On a server we run ourselves. Your Google sign-in token is stored encrypted, and only the server can read it. Calendar contents and tokens are never written to logs. The database is backed up every night onto the same server, and each backup is deleted after 14 days. There are no other copies.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data or share it with anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Oiko's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

You can remove Oiko's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When you disconnect in the app, or ask us by email, we delete the stored token, the calendars you chose, the email address and the calendar data we kept. If Google ends access on its own, we delete the same, but keep the email address so the app can ask you to connect again. Time away that you confirmed stays in the family's plan until you delete it. Because each backup is deleted after 14 days, deleted data is gone from the backups about two weeks later.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
